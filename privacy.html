1. Who we are

Hayso Health Limited, a company registered in the United Kingdom, is the data controller for the Hayso app and haysohealth.com. This means we decide how and why your personal data is used.

Contact our privacy team at privacy@haysohealth.com.

This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

3. Your health data

Information about your health is "special category data" under UK GDPR. It gets extra protection.

  • We only use it with your explicit consent, which you give in the app when you set up. Nothing is pre-ticked.
  • We only use it to run Hayso for you: your logs, plan, progress, reminders, hayso's replies, and sharing with a clinic if you choose.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time in Profile, then Who sees my data. Hayso then stops storing new health logs. To remove everything, delete your account.
  • We do not use your health data for advertising, and we never sell it.

4. Our legal reasons

  • Contract: to provide the app and the account you signed up for.
  • Explicit consent: to use your health data, to share with your clinic, and to send you news and offers if you opt in.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep Hayso secure, check it is working, fix bugs and improve it in ways you would expect. You can object to this (see section 10).
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or share data.

5. Sharing with your clinic

We only share with a clinic you have linked using its code, and only what you choose to share. Your clinic can then see the logs, progress and messages you share, and questions hayso passes on.

Your clinic is a separate controller for what it receives and is responsible for how it uses it in your care. You can turn sharing off or unlink at any time. Information already shared stays in the clinic's records under the clinic's own policy.

7. How we use AI

  • When you chat with hayso, we send your message, your first name and a short summary of today's plan and score to Anthropic to write a reply. We do not send your email or account details.
  • When you photograph a meal, the photo is sent to Anthropic to estimate what is in it.
  • Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. It may keep it for a short time for safety and misuse checks, under its commercial terms.
  • AI never makes decisions about your treatment, and nothing AI produces has a legal or similar effect on you.
  • We also use AI to summarise public health web pages, such as NHS pages, for our information library. No personal data is used for this.

8. Data outside the UK

Your Hayso account and logs are stored in Ireland, in the EU. UK law treats the EU as giving an adequate level of protection.

Some providers are in the USA or may access data from there. Where that happens we use safeguards allowed by UK law, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum. Email us for a copy.

9. How long we keep data

  • Your account and logs: while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account: we delete your data within 30 days. Backups clear within a further 35 days.
  • App usage and errors: 60 days.
  • App health checks: 30 days.
  • Consent records and complaints: as long as we need them to show we acted properly, up to 6 years.
  • Clinic copies: anything already shared with your clinic stays in its records under its own policy.
  • We keep data longer only if the law requires it.

10. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the data we hold about you;
  • download it: Profile, then Settings, then Download my data;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it: Profile, then Settings, then Delete account, or see Delete your account;
  • restrict or object to some uses, including anything based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, email privacy@haysohealth.com. It is free. We will reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm who you are first.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please complain to us first at privacy@haysohealth.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it, keep you updated, and tell you the outcome.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

  • Website: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
  • Phone: 0303 123 1113
  • Post: Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF

12. Security

Your data is encrypted when it travels and when it is stored. Access is limited to the people who need it, and admin access is logged. Each account can only reach its own data. If a breach puts you at risk, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

13. Children

Hayso is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has used Hayso, email us and we will delete their data.

14. Emails and notifications

We send emails you need, such as sign-in codes and important account notices. We only send news and offers if you opt in, and every one has an unsubscribe link. You can turn app notifications on or off in the app or in your phone's settings.

15. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when Hayso or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. We will tell you about important changes in the app or by email before they take effect.

Our story

A prescription is only the start of your treatment

Hayso began when friends of our founder started treatment. They had a consultation and a prescription, then very little support.

Questions about food, side effects and life after treatment had nowhere to go. So we built Hayso around the person on treatment, to make the whole journey clearer, safer and better supported.

Before you startA plan, your daily targets and a guide to the first four weeks.
During treatmentSimple daily logging, a weekly check-in and your clinic in the loop.
After treatmentProtein, strength and a weekly weigh-in to keep your progress.

Ask Hayso your questions

See all answers
Who is Hayso for?
Hayso is for adults on prescribed weight management treatment, whether you are starting soon, on treatment now, or have finished and want to keep your progress. You need to be 18 or over.
Do I need a clinic to use Hayso?
No. Hayso works on its own. If your clinic uses Hayso, you can enter their code and choose what to share with them.
Does Hayso give medical advice?
No. Hayso helps you log your day, gives general tips on food and movement, and passes on what you report to your clinic if you choose. Your prescriber makes all decisions about your treatment.
Who can see my data?
You can see everything you log. Your clinic only sees what you choose to share. We never sell your data or use it for adverts, and it is stored securely in the EU.
How much does Hayso cost?
You can try Hayso free, then choose a monthly or yearly plan. Some clinics cover the cost for their patients.
What happens when I stop treatment?
Hayso moves you onto a plan for life after treatment, with protein, strength sessions and a weekly weigh-in to help you keep your progress.
When can I download Hayso?
Hayso is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Join the list at the bottom of this page and we will email you the moment it is live.

4. Health information, not medical advice

Please read this section carefully. It explains what Hayso is and what it is not.

What Hayso is

Hayso is a wellbeing and logging app for adults who are before, on, or after a weight management treatment such as GLP-1 medication. It helps you log meals, water, activity, symptoms and treatment days, see your progress, and get general information about food, movement and habits.

What Hayso is not

  • Hayso is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any disease or condition.
  • Hayso does not prescribe, supply or recommend any medicine, and it never gives advice on doses, starting, stopping or changing treatment.
  • Hayso is not a doctor, nurse, pharmacist, dietitian or personal trainer, and it does not replace one.
  • Hayso is not an emergency or monitoring service. Nobody watches your logs around the clock.

Everything is general information

All content in Hayso is general information, written for a general audience. This includes your Hayso plan, Hayso score, targets, reminders, meal ideas, recipes, workouts, articles, progress charts, food and nutrition estimates, and every reply from hayso, our in-app guide.

Hayso uses the answers you give to choose and arrange this information. That does not make it medical advice or a personal recommendation. We use information-only language on purpose: words like "aim for", "try" or "you could" describe general options, not instructions for you.

You are responsible for your own decisions

  • How you understand and use the information in Hayso is your own choice and your own responsibility.
  • Always follow the advice of your doctor, prescriber or clinic. Speak to them before you change your treatment, diet, fluid intake or activity, and if anything in Hayso does not seem right for you.
  • Never ignore professional medical advice, or delay getting it, because of something you read or logged in Hayso.
  • If you have a health condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, have had an eating disorder, or are unsure whether exercise is safe for you, get advice from a health professional before you follow any meal idea or workout.

Food and nutrition

  • Calories, protein and other nutrition figures are estimates, including those made from photos or voice notes. They can be wrong. Check them before you rely on them.
  • Recipes and meal ideas may contain allergens. Always check ingredients and food labels yourself. Hayso cannot guarantee a meal is safe for your allergies or intolerances.

Exercise

  • Exercise carries a risk of injury. Work at your own level, warm up, and use equipment safely.
  • Stop straight away and get help if you feel pain, chest tightness, dizziness, faintness or severe breathlessness.
  • Suggested weights, reps and effort levels are general starting points only.

Getting help

  • In an emergency, call 999.
  • For urgent medical advice, call NHS 111 or visit 111.nhs.uk.
  • If you are struggling with your mental health, you can call Samaritans free on 116 123, any time.
  • Questions about your medicine or symptoms should go to your prescriber, pharmacist or GP.

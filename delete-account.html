Delete your account

For the Hayso app by Hayso Health Limited. Last updated: 17 November 2026

In the app

  1. Open Hayso and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email, no app needed

Email privacy@haysohealth.com with the subject Delete my Hayso account. Send it from the email address you use for Hayso. If you signed in with Apple or Google, tell us which. We may ask you to confirm it is you, then we will delete your account and tell you when it is done.

Cancel your subscription too

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, or you may still be charged.

What we delete, and when

  • Deleted: your account, profile, setup answers, all logs (weight, meals, photos, water, symptoms, treatment, workouts), messages with hayso, feedback and consent choices.
  • When: within 30 days of your request. Backups clear within a further 35 days.
  • Kept by your clinic: anything you already shared with a linked clinic stays in that clinic's records under its own policy.
  • Kept by us only if needed: a record that you asked us to delete your account, and anything the law requires us to keep, such as records of a complaint.

Want a copy first? In the app go to Profile, then Settings, then Download my data before you delete. See our Privacy Policy for more.
